# Runly — Simple Software for Small Service Businesses

Runly is a lightweight business operating system built for small service teams (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, and contractors).

It helps you manage your entire workflow — from quote to job to invoice to payment — without juggling multiple tools.

👉 https://runly.so

---

## 🚧 The Problem

Most small service businesses run on a mix of:

- QuickBooks for invoicing  
- Spreadsheets or whiteboards for scheduling  
- Text messages and calls for coordination  

This creates:
- missed follow-ups  
- slow invoicing  
- unclear job status  
- unnecessary admin work  

---

## ✅ The Solution

Runly simplifies everything into one clear workflow:

**Quote → Job → Invoice → Payment**

No complex setup. No bloated features. Just what small teams actually need.

---

## 🔑 Core Features

- Create and send quotes quickly  
- Schedule jobs and assign work  
- Track job progress  
- Generate invoices in seconds  
- Track payments without extra tools  
- Keep customer and job data in one place  

---
